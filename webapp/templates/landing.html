Pipeline

From photo to fix list

One straight path from a road photo to an actionable maintenance report.

01

Capture

Upload a photo from any phone browser — no app install needed.

02

Detect

A YOLOv8 model trained on RDD2022 locates cracks and potholes with bounding boxes.

03

Score

Bounding-box area relative to frame size drives a Low/Medium/High severity rating.

04

Report

Results render instantly with labeled boxes, ready to log or forward to a maintenance team.

Taxonomy

What it recognizes

Four RDD2022-standard damage classes, the same taxonomy used in the Global Road Damage Detection Challenge.

D00

Longitudinal crack

Runs parallel to the road's direction — usually early-stage surface fatigue.

D10

Transverse crack

Cuts across the road — often thermal or load-related stress.

D20

Alligator crack

Interconnected cracking in a scale-like pattern — a sign of structural fatigue.

D40

Pothole

A bowl-shaped void in the surface — the highest-priority repair category.
